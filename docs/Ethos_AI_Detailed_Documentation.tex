\begin{abstract}
In Bangladesh, over 70,000–90,000 students seek foreign higher education opportunities annually. In the absence of a centralized, mandatory regulatory framework for overseas education consultancies, students and their families are frequently subjected to exploitative practices, including exorbitant non-refundable upfront fees, opaque contractual clauses, forged acceptance letters, and fabricated visa outcomes. 

\textbf{Ethos AI} is an enterprise-grade trust-and-payments platform engineered to systematically eliminate informational asymmetry and financial exploitation within the Bangladeshi study-abroad sector. The platform synthesizes five foundational pillars: (1) a verified consultancy directory with government licensing validation, (2) an itemized side-by-side fee comparison engine, (3) a milestone-based escrow payment system with integer-level currency precision and an immutable cryptographic audit ledger, (4) dual-engine AI-driven document fraud detection (OCR-based offer letter authenticity analysis and smart contract fine-print parsing), and (5) a synchronized parent-student guardian monitoring hub. This document provides a complete technical specification of Ethos AI, spanning architectural design, data models, microservice interactions, cryptographic security protocols, and empirical evaluation metrics.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and Problem Domain}
% -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Market Context in Bangladesh}
Higher education mobility has escalated dramatically in Bangladesh over the past decade. Annually, tens of thousands of applicants target universities in North America, Western Europe, and Australasia. However, the consulting sector remains fragmented and predominantly unregulated. A typical consulting contract demands upfront deposits ranging from \text{BDT 50,000} to upwards of \text{BDT 300,000} before any university evaluation or visa assessment has occurred.

\subsection{Primary Failure Modes in Current Consulting Workflows}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}]
    \item \textbf{Predatory Advance Fee Extraction:} Consultancies collect non-refundable service fees before submitting applications, shifting all financial risk onto students and parents.
    \item \textbf{Opaque Fee Structures \& Hidden Penalties:} Contracts lack transparent itemization, burying clauses that forfeit deposits even in the event of institutional rejections or visa bans.
    \item \textbf{Offer Letter Forgery:} Malicious agencies fabricate acceptance letters using modified graphic templates to extract milestone payments from unsuspecting clients.
    \item \textbf{Informational Disenfranchisement of Guardians:} While parents frequently finance the entirety of the study-abroad budget, they are routinely excluded from direct communication, lacking visibility into file status, verified documents, and actual escrow balances.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=lightgraybg,colframe=primaryblue,title=\textbf{The Ethos AI Mission}]
To establish a cryptographically verifiable, economically safeguarded, and AI-audited ecosystem that transforms student-agency interactions from adversarial transactions into transparent, milestone-driven partnerships.
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{Architectural Components}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Next.js 16 Application Layer (Port 3000):} Utilizes Turbopack, React 19 Server/Client Components, Route Handlers, and Neon Auth. It orchestrates user authentication, role-based access control (RBAC), milestone escrow workflows, and responsive UI rendering using custom Neubrutalist design tokens.
    \item \textbf{FastAPI AI Microservice (Port 8001):} An asynchronous Python 3.12 microservice running on Uvicorn. It hosts the OCR document authenticity scanner, the NLP agreement clause classifier, the heuristic agency scam risk engine, and the AI study counselor.
    \item \textbf{Neon Serverless PostgreSQL Database:} Relational data layer managing 17+ relational models with strict referential integrity, foreign key cascading, and database connection pooling.
    \item \textbf{Encrypted S3 Private Document Vault:} Secure object store accessed exclusively through authenticated streaming endpoints, ensuring zero public exposure of personal student records.
\end{itemize}

\section{Core Feature Modules \& Technical Implementation}
% -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Module 5.1: Multi-Role Authentication, RBAC \& Guardian Linking}
Ethos AI enforces strict role separation across four distinct personas:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Student:} Manages personal academic profile, uploads documents, deposits milestone escrow funds, and communicates with verified agencies.
    \item \textbf{Parent:} Possesses a synchronized guardian dashboard linked via a unique alphanumeric identifier (\texttt{linkCode}). Receives non-technical Bengali status summaries and milestone release notifications.
    \item \textbf{Agency:} Reviews applicant submissions, submits formal stage updates, registers official fee benchmarks, and requests milestone disbursements.
    \item \textbf{Admin:} Exercises platform-wide governance, audits government agency licenses, mediates escrow disputes, and broadcasts security alerts.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Module 5.2–5.4: Consultancy Directory, Pricing \& Side-by-Side Comparison}
The agency directory surfaces verified listings audited against the Ministry of Education Bangladesh (\texttt{MOE-BD-*}) registry. The comparison engine allows simultaneous side-by-side inspection of up to 4 agencies across:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Historical Visa Success Rate (audited via embassy submissions).
    \item Minimum and Maximum Fee Ranges in Bangladeshi Poisha.
    \item Refund turn-around guarantee periods (typically 7–30 business days).
    \item AI-calculated Scam Risk Scores (0–100 scale).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Module 5.5–5.6: Application Lifecycle State Machine \& Document Vault}
Applications progress through an immutable, seven-stage state machine:
\[
\text{SUBMITTED} \longrightarrow \text{UNDER\_REVIEW} \longrightarrow \text{OFFER\_RECEIVED} \longrightarrow \text{PAYMENT\_PENDING} \longrightarrow \text{VISA\_PROCESSING} \longrightarrow \text{VISA\_APPROVED} \longrightarrow \text{COMPLETED}
\]
Each state transition requires actor validation and is recorded in the \texttt{StageEvent} table with a cryptographic timestamp and actor ID. Document uploads are stored with encrypted storage pointers, MIME validation, and stream-only downloads.

\subsection{Module 5.7: Milestone Escrow Payment System \& Cryptographic Hash Ledger}
To eradicate upfront fee exploitation, funds are split across milestones:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{File Assessment Milestone:} 15--20\% released upon certified institutional application.
    \item \textbf{Offer Letter Verification Milestone:} 30--40\% held in escrow until the AI OCR scanner and student confirm genuine unconditional offer issuance.
    \item \textbf{Visa Lodging Milestone:} Balance held until visa submission confirmation.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Poisha-Level Currency Arithmetic}
All financial transactions are calculated strictly in integer Poisha ($1\text{ BDT} = 100\text{ Poisha}$) using 64-bit integer structures (\texttt{BigInt}) to completely prevent floating-point rounding errors:
\begin{equation}
\text{TotalEscrowPoisha} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \text{MilestoneAmountPoisha}_i
\end{equation}

\subsubsection{Cryptographic Audit Ledger}
Every ledger event (\texttt{HOLD}, \texttt{RELEASE}, \texttt{REFUND}, \texttt{DISPUTE\_FREEZE}) generates an immutable SHA-256 transaction hash:
\begin{equation}
\text{txHash}_k = \text{SHA256}\left(\text{txHash}_{k-1} \,\|\, \text{milestoneId} \,\|\, \text{amountPoisha} \,\|\, \text{actorId} \,\|\, \text{timestamp}\right)
\end{equation}

\section{Artificial Intelligence \& Machine Learning Pipeline}
% -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Offer Letter Authenticity OCR Scanner (Module 5.8)}
When an agency or student uploads an admission offer letter (PDF or image), the microservice invokes PyMuPDF and Tesseract OCR to perform:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{University Domain \& Accreditation Matching:} Cross-references institutional names against accredited higher education registries.
    \item \textbf{Layout \& Visual Heuristics Analysis:} Detects font mismatch anomalies, digital artifacting, and inconsistent logo resolutions indicative of tampering.
    \item \textbf{Risk Probability Scoring:} Assigns a continuous risk score $S \in [0, 100]$:
    \begin{equation}
    S_{\text{forgery}} = w_1 \cdot f_{\text{domain}} + w_2 \cdot f_{\text{text\_anomalies}} + w_3 \cdot f_{\text{metadata}}
    \end{equation}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Smart Agreement Clause Analyzer (Module 5.9)}
Consultancy agreements are parsed using an NLP classification pipeline that extracts and classifies individual legal clauses into:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Predatory Clauses:} E.g., ``Total fee is 100\% non-refundable under any circumstance including university rejection.''
    \item \textbf{Ambiguous Terms:} Hidden administrative or processing fees without defined ceilings.
    \item \textbf{Plain Language Summaries:} Translates legalese into accessible English and Bengali summaries for students and parents.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{AI Study Counselor \& Global Scholar Finder (Modules 5.11–5.13)}
Utilizes multi-parameter vector matching to map student profiles (CGPA, IELTS band, target field, budget constraints) to over 250+ university programs across Canada, the United Kingdom, Germany, the United States, and Australia.
